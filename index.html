ForewordPage 01

Dear Clara,

Today is your day.

So i wanted to make something different, a book. Not just a book, but something to remind you of how much you are loved, and how much you mean to the people around you.

Contains a collection of small things i noticed about you, and a few words from the people who love you.

This book is only a beginning. There will always be more to notice, more to remember, and more reasons to celebrate you.
